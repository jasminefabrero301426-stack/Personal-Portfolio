SYSTEM ANALYSIS PROJECT

Payroll System

This Payroll System is designed to manage employee information and calculate their salaries efficiently. It has three main functions: Payroll, Register Employee, and View Employee.

The Payroll section allows users to enter employee details, pay period dates, regular hours, and overtime hours to calculate and save payroll entries. The Register Employee section collects important employee information, such as ID, name, TIN, SSS, PhilHealth, department, and position. Lastly, the View Employee section displays registered employee information and their payroll records.

The system also includes input validation to prevent incorrect information and ensure accurate records. Every screen returns to the Main Menu, making the system easy to navigate and use.
